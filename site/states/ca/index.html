What this data says

California reported 1,096 homicides in the trailing 12 months — a rate of 2.8 per 100,000 residents. That's a 29.5% decrease compared to the prior 12-month window.

California's rate sits near the national median. It ranks #28 of 51 states by homicides per capita (1 = highest). The national median across all states is 3.2 per 100,000.

Other notable year-over-year shifts: rape is down 25.5%, robbery is down 30.2%, aggravated assault is down 19.2%.

All figures are pulled directly from the FBI's Crime Data Explorer, which aggregates monthly submissions from local law enforcement agencies. Numbers reflect offenses reported to police — not all crime, and not crime that was solved. Rate calculations use 2024 U.S. Census Bureau population estimates.
