THE REPORTING GAP

When a police department stops reporting, that's the story.

FBI reporting is voluntary. After the bureau retired its old SRS system in 2021 and switched fully to NIBRS, many large agencies failed to make the transition — and have not submitted national crime data since. Some agencies issue self-reported numbers in press releases while sending nothing to the FBI. This site exists to show both.

How this works

01

FBI Crime Data Explorer

We pull from the FBI's official Crime Data Explorer API for all 50 states and the District of Columbia, covering eight major offense categories from 1985 to present.

02

Continuous monitoring

An automated process checks the FBI's data daily and pulls fresh records as soon as the bureau updates. Every scrape is logged and auditable.

03

Gap detection

When agencies stop reporting, we flag them. When agencies issue press releases that conflict with their FBI submissions, that becomes a public record.
